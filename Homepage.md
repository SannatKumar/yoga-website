# Homepage Content Plan – Yoga Practice Website

---

## Purpose

To create a compelling, thoughtful, and visually clean homepage that welcomes visitors with a philosophical essence and guides them to explore yoga through structured, multilingual content.

---

## Hero Section: Namaste & Welcome

**Position:** Immediately below the navbar  
**Goal:** Establish connection, cultural authenticity, and calm vibe

- Text:
  - Heading: "Namaste 🙏 & Welcome to Your Yoga Journey"
  - Subtext: “Discover the ancient path to inner peace, strength, and clarity through conscious practice.”

- Visual:
  - Calm background (sunrise, mandala, nature)
  - Center-aligned text
  - Mobile-first scaling font sizes

---

## Philosophical WH Section: “What, Why, When, How, Where Yoga?”

**Position:** Immediately after Hero section  
**Goal:** Spark curiosity, communicate depth, and frame the site’s intention

- **Layout:**
  - Use of large, centered text blocks or cards
  - Icons for each question (optional)
  - Fade-in or scroll animation for emphasis

- **Cards / Blocks:**

| Question | Content Snippet |
|----------|-----------------|
| **What is Yoga?** | Yoga means union—of body, breath, and being. |
| **Why Yoga?** | To awaken clarity, balance, health, and awareness. |
| **When Yoga?** | Anytime. It is a way of life, not a schedule. |
| **How Yoga?** | With presence, breath, guidance, and stillness. |
| **Where Yoga?** | Wherever you are—in body and mind. |

> All sections are intentionally philosophical and minimal to allow the mind to reflect.

---

## Explore Section: "Explore Our Content"

**Position:** Below WH Section  
**Goal:** Guide users directly to core resources (call-to-action hub)

- **Title:** “Explore Our Content”
- **Layout:** 3 or 4 large cards/blocks with images/icons + brief descriptions
- **Links To:**
  - 🌿 *Practice Guide*: Step-by-step sequences
  - 🎥 *Videos*: Practice-along guided sessions
  - 🧘 *Asanas*: Learn postures with details
  - 📚 *Philosophy*: Wisdom, principles, and yogic thought

---

## Responsive Design Considerations

- **Mobile First:** Hero, WH cards, and explore sections stack vertically
- **Tablet/Desktop:** Grid-based layout, animated transitions (Tailwind-friendly)
- **Visual Balance:** Clean whitespace, readable font sizes, contrast

---

## Future Add-ons (optional)

- Site visitor count component (already implemented)
- “Start Now” button (links to guided sequence or onboarding)
- Quote rotator (simple yoga wisdom quotes)
- Newsletter/email sign-up (future engagement)

---

## Summary of Homepage Flow

1. **Navbar**
2. **Hero Section:** Greeting and welcome message
3. **WH Yoga Cards/Blocks**
4. **Explore Content Cards**
5. **Call-to-Action or Footer**

---

## Notes

- Tone: Philosophical yet welcoming
- Texts: Keep multilingual-ready with Laravel localization
- Focus: Inspire curiosity & offer guidance, not just info
